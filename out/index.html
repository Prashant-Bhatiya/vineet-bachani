Patient-First, Evidence-Based Orthopedic Care

Dr. Vineet Bachani is a qualified medical practitioner holding an MS in Orthopedic Surgery with rich clinical and surgical experience from top-tier institutions across India. Having trained under legendary surgeons including Dr. (Prof.) Anil Arora (Max Super Speciality Hospital) and Dr. Vikram Shah (Shalby Hospital), he brings cutting-edge surgical precision and international recovery protocols to his practice in South Bopal, Ahmedabad.

His philosophy focuses on joint preservation and conservative management wherever possible. Surgery is only advised when non-invasive interventions are exhausted, ensuring patients regain mobility, reduce pain, and return to an active lifestyle safely and quickly.
